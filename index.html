Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our services.

What is the cost of pigeon safety nets? ▼
The cost depends on the area size and type of net required. Generally, pigeon safety nets start from ₹8–₹15 per sq. ft. for standard HDPE nets. We provide free site visits and transparent quotations with no hidden charges. Contact us for an accurate estimate for your property.
How long does installation take? ▼
Installation time varies by project size. A standard balcony net can be installed in 2–4 hours. Multi-floor apartment nets may take 1–3 days. We always provide a timeline estimate before starting work and ensure minimal disruption to your daily routine.
Are invisible grills safe for children? ▼
Yes, absolutely. Our invisible grills are made from SS316 marine-grade stainless steel cables with a tested breaking load of over 300 kg per cable. They are BIS-certified and designed to withstand impact. Thousands of families across Chennai trust our invisible grills for child and pet safety.
Do you provide warranty? ▼
Yes! We provide a 1-year warranty on all our installations covering any defects in materials or workmanship. Our HDPE nets are UV-stabilized and typically last 3–5 years. Invisible grills have a 5-year structural warranty with proper care. We also offer free maintenance visits during the warranty period.
